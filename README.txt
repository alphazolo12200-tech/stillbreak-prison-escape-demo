STILLBREAK – Prison Escape (FREE DEMO, levels 1-2)
=================================================

A 3D first-person prison-escape game. Time crawls when you stand still and runs when you move.
This demo covers levels 1 and 2 only. The full game has 10 levels and the escape ending.

HOW TO HOST ON GITHUB PAGES
  Put index.html in the repository root (or /docs), then enable Settings > Pages. An internet connection is needed because three.js loads from cdnjs.

CONTROLS
  WASD / arrow keys move  |  Mouse or IJKL look  |  Click or SPACE fire/stab
  1-5 switch weapons  |  E interact  |  SHIFT dash  |  Q ghost  |  F shockwave  |  R rage  |  C shield
  Mobile: on-screen joystick (left), drag right side to look, on-screen buttons.
